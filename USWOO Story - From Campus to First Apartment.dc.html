Studying at NYU, and ending up renting in New Jersey

From insisting on Manhattan to discovering Jersey City, Ethan’s first off-campus lease changed how he thought about commuting and living in New York.

Jun 12, 20251.5 min read

Before the end of their freshman year, Ethan and his friend had already started planning to move out of the NYU dorms.

Their plan seemed simple: find an apartment in Manhattan, split the rent, and stay as close to campus as possible.

After weeks of searching, reality turned out harsher than expected.

Apartments within budget were either too small or in poor condition. When they finally found one they liked, it was already rented out before they could book a viewing.

Worse still, rent was only the beginning. Utilities, internet and move-in costs added up to much more than they had budgeted each month.

As the dorm checkout date drew closer, they still hadn’t decided which neighborhood to live in.

“Just because you study in New York, do you have to live in New York?”

After contacting USWOO, the agent didn’t send a pile of listings right away. Instead, they asked about their budget, class schedules and commuting habits.

At the end of the conversation, the agent suddenly asked: “Have you considered Jersey City?”

Ethan’s first reaction was to say no. “I go to school in New York. Why would I live in New Jersey?”

But when the agent put listings in Manhattan and Jersey City side by side, comparing prices and transit routes, he began to waver.

It turned out that from some PATH stations in Jersey City, getting to Manhattan wasn’t nearly as complicated as he had imagined.

And at a similar budget, they might be able to find a more spacious apartment with better amenities.

He had thought he was choosing between budget and comfort, but there was a third option he hadn’t considered.

What changed his mind was the viewing

On the weekend, Ethan and his friend decided to visit Jersey City in person.

Walking out of the PATH station and into several apartment buildings they had never looked at before, they realized that life across the river was very different from what they had imagined.

Bright common spaces, a gym, and some apartments with views of the Manhattan skyline.

Of course, the agent also reminded them not to judge a place by looks alone.

How long was the walk from the apartment to PATH? Was it easy to get home at night? After factoring in transportation costs, how much could they actually save each month?

After comparing several listings and complete commuting routes, they finally chose an apartment near a PATH station.

Not because it was the most luxurious, but because budget, commute and living conditions finally struck the balance they wanted.

Living in New York and living in New York are two different things

After moving to Jersey City, Ethan still went to NYU for class as usual, and spent weekends with friends in Manhattan.

The only difference was that on the way home each day, he could see the familiar New York skyline across the river.

A few months earlier, he had thought living in New Jersey was a compromise.

Looking back now, he is actually glad someone asked him a question at the time:

“Why does it have to be Manhattan?”

Sometimes finding a better home isn’t about seeing more apartments. It’s about having someone help you see options you never considered.

大一结束前，Ethan 和朋友已经开始计划搬出 NYU 宿舍。

两人想得很简单：在 Manhattan 找套公寓，平摊房租，最好离学校近一点。

结果找了几个星期，现实比想象中残酷。

预算内的房子不是太小，就是条件一般。好不容易看中一套，刚准备预约看房，就发现已经租出去了。

更崩溃的是，房租只是开始。水电、网络、各种入住费用加起来，每个月的开销比预计高出不少。

宿舍退房日期越来越近，他们却连住哪个区都没决定。

“谁说在纽约上学，就一定要住纽约？”

联系 USWOO 后，Agent 没有立刻发来一堆房源，而是先问了他们的预算、课程安排和通勤习惯。

聊到最后，Agent 突然问了一句：

“你们考虑过 Jersey City 吗？”

Ethan 第一反应是拒绝。

“我在 NYU 上学，为什么要住 New Jersey？”

但当 Agent 把 Manhattan 和 Jersey City 的房源、价格、交通路线放在一起比较时，他开始动摇了。

原来，从 Jersey City 的部分 PATH 车站出发，到 Manhattan 并没有想象中那么麻烦。

而且在相近预算下，他们可能有机会租到空间更宽敞、设施更完善的公寓。

原本以为自己是在预算和居住体验之间二选一，没想到还有第三种选择。

真正让他改主意的，是那次看房

周末，Ethan 和朋友决定去 Jersey City 实地看看。

从 PATH 车站出来，走进几栋之前从没关注过的公寓，他们才发现，河对岸的生活和想象中很不一样。

明亮的公共空间、健身房，还有部分公寓能看到的 Manhattan Skyline。

当然，Agent 也提醒他们，不能只看房子漂亮。

从公寓到 PATH 要走多久？晚上回家方不方便？算上交通费，每个月实际能省多少？

比较了几套房源和完整的通勤路线后，他们最终选定了一套靠近 PATH 的公寓。

不是因为它最豪华，而是因为预算、通勤和居住条件，终于达到了他们想要的平衡。

原来，住在纽约和生活在纽约，是两回事

搬到 Jersey City 后，Ethan 还是照常去 NYU 上课，周末和朋友在 Manhattan 聚会。

只不过，现在每天回家的路上，他会从河对岸看见熟悉的纽约天际线。

几个月前，他还觉得住在 New Jersey 是一种妥协。

现在回头看，他反而庆幸当时有人多问了一句：

“为什么一定要住 Manhattan？”

有时候，找到更合适的家，不是看了更多房子，而是有人帮你看到了原本没考虑过的选择。

注：本文为根据常见学生租房经历创作的情景案例，人物与具体经历均为虚构。
